General Purpose Ball End Mills

General purpose ball end mills are versatile cutting tools used in milling operations to remove material from a workpiece. Ball end mills have a rounded tip, which is ideal for creating curved surfaces, contours, and 3D shapes, and are used for shallow slotting, contour milling and pocketing applications. They are designed to handle a variety of materials and machining tasks, making them a good choice for many general machining applications.

Solid Carbide 4-Flute Centercutting Single End AlTiN

Solid Carbide end mills provide superior wear resistance and can withstand the highest cutting temperatures offering a higher degree of accuracy over other materials. Four or more flutes allow for faster feed rates and produce a finer finish than two and three flute tools. Centercutting design allows for plunge milling into solid materials with good chip removal capability. The AlTiN coating maintains high surface hardness at elevated temperatures improving tool life and allowing faster feed rates in ferrous materials.

About Ball End Mills

Ball end mills, also known as ball nose end mills, are specialized cutting tools used for milling complex contoured surfaces, slotting, and pocketing. A ball end mill is constructed with a rounded cutting edge featuring a radius equal to half the tool diameter. This unique geometry makes them indispensable in the machining of high-precision dies, molds, and 3D surfacing applications where a smooth, curved finish is required.
